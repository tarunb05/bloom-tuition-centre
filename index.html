How We Teach

No rote learning. We take each concept apart until it makes sense, then practise it until it's yours. Marks follow understanding, not the other way round.

The why comes first

Every rule, formula and fact gets explained before it's used, so students know where it comes from and when it applies.

Questions are the lesson

"Why?" is welcome at any point in the class. Doubts get cleared on the spot, not saved up until exam week.

Practice that checks thinking

Problems that test whether the idea has landed, not just whether the steps were memorised.
